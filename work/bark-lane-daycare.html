OUR PROCESS

How we built it

The project combined a clear service story, boutique art direction and a simple route from discovery to enquiry.

1) Strategy + structure

Mapped the page flow around search intent and conversions: services, trust, daycare experience, and quick enquiry routes.

2) Luxury visual direction

Dark, warm, boutique styling with soft glows and clean typography — designed to feel premium without slowing performance.

3) Feature sections

Built clear “what you get” sections and the school bus pickups block — reducing questions and improving conversion clarity.

4) Performance polish

Optimised assets and interactions to keep the site snappy. Premium motion, minimal scripts, stable layout.

5) SEO foundations

SEO-ready page structure, clean headings, and schema-friendly layout — built to support local rankings and future content.
