SEO foundations / London

Help the right customers
understand the offer.

London intent can be broad or highly specific. We establish the services and locations you genuinely want to target, then map useful pages to those needs. We avoid fabricated London offices, unsupported local reviews and interchangeable borough pages. Technical structure, clear headings and relevant internal links support the content.

We consider crawlability, page performance and accessible navigation from the start. Technical setup supports useful content; it does not replace it or guarantee a position in search.

Good questions / clear answers

Planning a website in London?

Do you have a London office?

Our studio is based in Epping, Essex. We work with London businesses through an agreed remote delivery and communication process. This page describes the service area, not a separate London office.

Can you work with an existing London brand identity?

Yes. We can build around an established visual identity and content direction, or agree what needs to be refined before website design and development begin.

Can you plan a redesign around existing search visibility?

We review important URLs, content and the current structure before recommending changes. Redirects and migration checks are planned where needed, but rankings cannot be guaranteed during or after a redesign.
