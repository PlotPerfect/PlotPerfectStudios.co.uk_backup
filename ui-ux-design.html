PROCESS

From unclear screens to a build-ready system.

  1. Define the task: what visitors need to understand, decide or complete.
  2. Map the flow: organise screens and sections around that task.
  3. Set the direction: establish typography, colour, components and interaction patterns.
  4. Refine responsively: make sure the experience remains clear across screen sizes.
  5. Build or hand over: continue into development or provide a coherent direction for implementation.
